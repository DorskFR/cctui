## How it works

Peer policy is enforced on the server: a session may talk to its parent, its children, members of its room, and sessions linked through [cctuiverse](/cctui/features/cctuiverse/). A room is the boundary a human draws by putting sessions together. Messages are delivered to the target harness as a user turn wrapped in a tagged envelope that says who sent it; if the target is not running, the message waits and is delivered when it starts.
