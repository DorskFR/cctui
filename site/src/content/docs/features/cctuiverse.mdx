## How it works

It is on by default; `CCTUI_CCTUIVERSE=0` turns it off. The invite URL carries a link id, a bearer token and the inviter's key fingerprint. The joining server signs its half, the two exchange keys, and both sessions receive a `<cctuiverse-linked>` turn. Posts between them are signed per link and fanned out through each server's normal peer path, so transcripts, history sharing and room membership behave as they do for local peers.

The invite is a bearer secret for its 10 minutes: do not paste it where an agent, a bot or a log can read it.
