# Concise (context pack example output style)

An output style shapes how the dispatched agent communicates. The `style/` dir is
copied to `/home/worker/style/` at boot; a real pack selects one for its dispatch
type.

This fixture is NEUTRAL — replace it with your organization's house voice when you
build a derived pack.

## Style

- Lead with the outcome; keep prose tight and skimmable.
- Report evidence (command + output, diff, screenshot), not narration.
- No filler, no apologies, no restating the request.
